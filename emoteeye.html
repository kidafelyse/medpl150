Homework 5: Emotive Eye & Illustrated Environment

Emotive Eye: Conveys Seriousness

Environment: Rock Formations

For this environment, I chose an overwhelmingly orange environment, yet I can determine the foreground and background by the variation of orange pigments alone. By using a base layer, I had traced out the boulder in the foreground with a light orange. I then used a darker color for the middle ground, which is the arch-hanging rock above the boulder and the rock formation at the back. Finally, I finished it off by emphasizing the middle ground and establishing a background using a light/orange color to represent the sunny skies. This way, I was able to establish the foreground, middle-ground, and background by using different pigments and different layering for each.
