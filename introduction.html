Meet Bailey! Are you ready to accept the quest?

You were never allowed to have dogs growing up. Partly because you have a "history of negligence" as your parents like to claim. Neither your friends nor your partner live with you yet, so you end up as a lonely adult years down the line. Luckily, your friend Lily is having you babysit her foster dog Bailey, a sweet-looking corgi.

Lily notices you looking excited at Bailey. So you remark, "I always wanted a corgi, it's been a life-long dream!". Lily thinks for a second and then gives you a challenge. She says that as long as you can take care of Bailey without any mishaps, she is willing to hand Bailey over to you on the spot.

You pet Bailey in the head and lead Bailey inside your apartment. Are you ready to give Bailey a forever home?
