Interactive Story Part 1

I am going to go for a narrative-driven, choice-based game where the gamer's decision will influence whether or not they get the best, intermediate, or worst ending. I will be applying the storyline of taking care of a dog. I'm thinking of maybe adding a variation of pets for the player to decide to take care of, such as dogs and cats, or simply just one dog.

Characters include the protagonist, who is the gamer, and Bailey, the dog. They both will go through different stages of the protagonist taking care of them, and based on the decisions of the narrator. There will be four endings. There is one good ending, where the pet is fed and has a place to relieve themselves. The intermediate endings are when the dog is either fed or unrelieved but not fed or relieved. The worst ending is when the dog is neither fed nor relieved.

The stakes? The protagonist has always wanted a pet, but due to her history of negligence, as shown in the challenges she faces in taking care of the dog, she has never had a pet. She gets the opportunity to have Bailey for free if she takes care of her perfectly. It is now up to the gamer to choose whether or not the protagonist gets to fulfill their dream, given the challenges that come up!
